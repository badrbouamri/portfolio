## Context

Two-month end-of-studies internship (PFA) at AIC Métallurgie (Delta Holding group), in Kénitra — a metal-forming site. The assigned scope covered the fleet of bending and rolling machines, whose availability directly determines whether production deadlines are met.

## Problem

The fleet of bending and rolling machines suffered recurring failures, causing unplanned production stoppages. The site had neither a structured analysis of these failures nor a formalised maintenance plan for this machine fleet.

## Objectives

- Identify the causes of recurring failures on the bending and rolling machines
- Calculate the rolling and unrolling forces specific to these machines
- Formalise a preventive and corrective maintenance plan
- Formulate recommendations to improve fleet reliability and reduce production stoppages

## My role

I carried out the failure diagnosis and the rolling/unrolling force calculations on my own. I proposed the preventive and corrective maintenance plan and the reliability recommendations, handed over to the site's maintenance team for validation and implementation.

## Approach & methodology

**Failure data collection** — gathering the history of stoppages and failures on the fleet's bending and rolling machines.

**Recurring failure analysis** — mechanical diagnosis of the failure modes observed on these machines.

**Rolling and unrolling force calculation** — sizing of the forces applied during bending and rolling operations, to objectively quantify the stresses the machine fleet is subjected to.

**Maintenance plan development** — building a preventive and corrective maintenance plan, and writing reliability-improvement recommendations.
