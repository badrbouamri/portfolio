## Context

Final-year internship (PFE) completed at Stellantis Kénitra, within the Micromobility workshop, over a six-month period. The workshop's transformation cost (TC) was drifting from its budget target, with a significant contribution from the scrap cost on line L2 and recurring sealing defects at station ST40, carried by a robotized adhesive-application and forming cell.

*Data has been deliberately indexed or anonymised out of respect for industrial confidentiality.*

## Problem

The workshop's transformation cost was drifting from its budget target. Two contributors were identified: a high scrap rate on line L2, and recurring sealing defects at station ST40, with no upstream detection or standardized rework mechanism in place.

## Objectives

- Identify the root causes of scrap on line L2 and on the robotized adhesive-application/forming cell.
- Reduce the scrap cost on line L2 and bring the transformation cost back toward its budget target.
- Make the treatment of sealing defects at station ST40 more reliable.
- Establish sustainable monitoring of scrap and associated consumables.

## My role

As a continuous improvement engineering intern, I carried the DMAIC approach end-to-end across the workshop's TC scope (scoping, diagnosis, implementation of actions, monitoring tools), under my industrial supervisor's oversight. I personally carried out the root-cause diagnosis, the design of the Power BI dashboard, and the development of the consumables-management web application.

## Approach & methodology

1. **Define** — Scoping of the perimeter (Micromobility workshop, line L2, station ST40) using a QQOQCCP and a SIPOC; formalizing the gap between the actual TC and its budget target.
2. **Measure** — Collection and structuring of scrap data; building a Pareto of defects to prioritize contributors to the transformation cost.
3. **Analyze** — Root-cause diagnosis via FMEA, an Ishikawa diagram, and 5 Whys, focused on the robotized adhesive-application/forming cell and on the sealing defects at station ST40.

> ⚠️ TO COMPLETE — the detail of the root-cause hypotheses tested and retained (from the PFE report).

4. **Improve** — Implementation of a standardized early-rework station to treat sealing defects upstream; deployment of corrective actions from the diagnosis.
5. **Control** — Design of a Power BI dashboard for scrap monitoring and a consumables-management web application, to sustain monitoring beyond the internship.
